# Terminology

This archive is organized around data tables first. Markdown files in this
directory provide orientation, while CSV files in `data/` hold structured
evidence.

## Data files

- `data/checked_tone.csv`: entries related to checked-tone readings.
- `data/literary_colloquial.csv`: literary and colloquial reading pairs.
- `data/nasal_finals.csv`: entries involving nasal finals.
- `data/initials.csv`: initial consonant inventory records.
- `vocabulary/core_words.csv`: general word-level vocabulary records.

## Terms

- Initial: the consonant or consonant-like segment at the beginning of a
  syllable.
- Final: the remainder of the syllable after the initial.
- Tone: the pitch category or pitch contour associated with a syllable.
- Checked tone: a tone category traditionally associated with syllables that
  historically ended in stop codas.
- Literary reading: a reading associated with literary, formal, or learned
  pronunciation.
- Colloquial reading: a reading associated with everyday spoken pronunciation.
- Nasal final: a final containing nasal resonance or a nasal coda.
